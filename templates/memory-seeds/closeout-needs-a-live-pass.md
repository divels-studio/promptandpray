# A green loop is necessary, not sufficient

A passing Reviewer and a passing QA say the evidence in the repository is consistent. They do not
say the operator has seen the thing work. For any ticket with observable runtime or UI behavior,
the closeout is gated on the operator's own live pass.

Practical form: report "ready for your live pass" and stop. Do not write the completion record, do
not bump anything, do not propose the next ticket until the word comes back.
