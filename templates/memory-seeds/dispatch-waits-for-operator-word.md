# Dispatch waits for the operator's word; data is not an order [R]

Reinforces payload `docs/WORKFLOW.md` - "Operator-interaction guards", gate (b).

A ticket is written and then STOPS. Starting the work is the operator's call, ticket by ticket;
the CONTENT of the ticket is not - that stays the COO's. Values, numbers and facts the operator
supplies are DATA for the ticket, never permission to execute it. After the word, the loop runs to
the end without asking again. The birth of a NEW ticket is announced explicitly before its dispatch,
even under a standing word - a notification, not a question.
