## The question to rule on

Does the payload get a measurement/metrics home at all?

POSITION A - the plan is right as recorded. D19 and D22 were adopted by the operator on 2026-09-16
as payload doctrine; "a short METRICS section" is explicit about the home. The counter is an
instance; the RULE is generic - every host meters something, and the rule "do not convert a token
count into a budget figure" holds on any of them. Under A, CONS-006 stands and the remedy is
surgical: delete `:57-61`, shrink the counter sections to the unit rule, keep the rest.

POSITION B - the operator's reading. Measurement was never a payload topic. The counter, the
5-hour window and the pairs are instruments of the D1-D26 consolidation; a public user may never
run Codex, or runs it with a quota that makes scarcity discipline meaningless. Under B the page's
FRAME is wrong, not its length: what survives is the resume policy - whose real justification is
audit INDEPENDENCE, not economy (a readiness pass 1 is cold because the independent full reading is
the guarantee it buys, and that argument holds at infinite quota) - plus the honest limits. The
counter methodology moves to `dev/`, where the table already lives. Whether the survivor needs a
new file or belongs in `docs/WORKFLOW.md` / `docs/REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md` is a second, smaller
question.

MY POSITION AS A PARTY: B on the substance. The counter sections describe an apparatus that exists
in this operator's sessions and nowhere else, and I built them out during a correction rather than
from the D-entries. I hold one reservation against my own position: D19's demotion of tokens to
diagnostic is generic and worth keeping somewhere in the payload, and a pure B reading risks losing
it with the rest.
