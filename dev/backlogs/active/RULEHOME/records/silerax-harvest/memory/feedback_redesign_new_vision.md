Import-wizard mockups must be a new way of working the step (flow, hierarchy, interaction - the big players' import flows),
carrying 100% of the code's functionality; never today's layout in new clothes. A reskin may be at most ONE of 2-3 radically
different variants.

**Why:** 2026-10-24 step 2 В1 (the code's order in the new shell) was cut at first sight: „Нов бардак със стари ....",
„нищо различно от сегашното, просто преоблечено". Step 1 lost a round because measures were taken after drawing.

**How to apply:** per step - deep code scan of the step (Sonnet) -> measures from the code BEFORE the first artboard -> 2-3
radical interactive variants with state tweaks -> the operator kills / merges / names a favourite -> change only that one,
only what is asked; product choices with 2+ options become tweaks. What won step 2: compact, everything „caught at a glance",
nothing jumps between lists, master-detail with a stable list, the operator's own idea (connector line), MetricsBar KPIs,
no DOS-like arrows. Code gaps found go into the redesign scope (§ 4a) with file:line; AI logic, post-processing and CRUD are
not touched without the operator's approval. Related: [[feedback-mockup-from-site]], [[project-import-redesign]].
